## What this licence does not cover
- **Source texts.** Records quote short excerpts from third-party sources, each linked to its original. Those sources keep
  their own copyright and terms; the excerpts are there to show where a fact comes from. Check the source before reusing it.
- **Photographs and images.** Each photo carries its own licence and credit (for example Wikimedia Commons terms) shown
  with it. They are not part of this data licence.
- **Map tiles and place-search data.** Basemap tiles (OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap contributors) and any pin looked up from
  OpenStreetMap, Wikidata or Amap keep their providers' terms; a pin records which source it came from.
- **Visitor contributions** are accepted under this same licence (see CONTRIBUTING.md).

The **software** (everything outside `data/` and the published records) is under the MIT licence in `LICENSE`.
